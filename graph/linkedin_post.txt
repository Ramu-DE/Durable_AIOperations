GRAPH ENGINEERING × BIOMEDICAL KNOWLEDGE GRAPH

AI was always going to arrive at graphs.

Not because graphs are fashionable — because knowledge has structure,
and structure is a graph.

Here is what that looks like applied to a real biomedical domain:

─────────────────────────────────────────
THREE GRAPHS. ONE SYSTEM.
─────────────────────────────────────────

Graph of Weights  (centre sphere)
The LLM — a dense mesh of learned associations.
Fast. Probabilistic. Excellent at pattern recall.
Cannot tell you *why* something is true.

Graph of Doing  (inner agentic cycle)
observe → assess → check → decide → act
The reasoning loop. The agent's executive function.
Connects model intuition to grounded action.

Graph of Knowing  (outer domain ring)
The formal knowledge layer.
For biomedical work, that means six entities:

  Drug  ·  Disease  ·  Clinical Trial
  Patient  ·  Gene  ·  Biomarker

...and nine provenance-tracked relationships:

  Drug        --[treats]------------>  Disease
  Biomarker   --[predicts response]->  Drug
  Gene        --[associated with]---->  Disease
  Patient     --[enrolled in]-------->  Clinical Trial
  Clinical Trial --[investigates]---->  Drug
  Clinical Trial --[studies]--------->  Disease
  Gene        --[serves as]---------->  Biomarker
  Biomarker   --[indicates]---------->  Disease
  Disease     --[triggers]----------->  Clinical Trial

Ring closed.
Two graphs. One walk.
Disease = universal hub.  Drug = commercial hub.

─────────────────────────────────────────
WHY THIS MATTERS FOR REGULATED ENVIRONMENTS
─────────────────────────────────────────

The Graph of Knowing is not a search index.
It is a formal semantic layer — versioned, SHACL-validated,
grounded in CDISC/SDTM terminology.

When an agent answers a question about a drug-disease relationship,
it is not retrieving a cached embedding.
It is walking a graph where every edge has a provenance trail
you can show an auditor.

"Self-improving" and "validated" are not synonyms.
Know which one your agent is actually querying.
